# Acceptance criteria (unapproved proposals)

- [ ] QS confirms the relevant revision identifiers and contractual scope references.
- [ ] Supervisor confirms the document is understandable and identifies discrepancies.
- [ ] No missing scope, dimension or construction method is invented by AI.
- [ ] No site activity is authorized on the basis of this draft.
- [ ] Accepted corrections are expressed as scoped rules and tested in a subsequent candidate summary.

Blocker: no actual construction documents or qualified reviewer approvals have been supplied.
